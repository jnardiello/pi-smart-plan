# Changelog

## 0.10.1

- **`plan_intent`'s confirmation form drops "Correct"**: only Confirm / Keep chatting remain. Keep chatting — and Esc, its exact equivalent — now explicitly REJECTS the objective (nothing created, no note dialog; corrections happen in the conversation itself), returning `isError:false` with a result telling the model the owner rejected it — keep chatting, re-elicit, and re-open `plan_intent` when it's right.
- **Mechanical floor under "the model never enters the plan machine"**: a session-local `investigationDone` flag latches the moment ANY tool runs (parent runs only) while pre-intent in `discovery`; from then on, a turn that closes in prose without `plan_intent` or `ask_smart_plan` is regenerated with a dedicated steer, instead of being silently legal because the discovery deliverable isn't ready yet. Pure chat with no tool calls stays free. Reset on guard off/on and on `plan_intent`'s Confirm.
- **No redundant "None of the above" on harness-composed forms**: `runAskForm` gains an `includeNoneOption` opt-out (default on, unchanged for model-composed `ask_smart_plan` forms); the Gate 1/2 review forms and the `plan_intent` Confirm/Correct form now suppress it, since they already have a complete, fixed option set — Esc/decline remains the sole postpone/nothing-created path. Objective statements confirmed via `plan_intent` are now capped at ~400 characters (`PlanStoreValidationError` guides distillation to WHAT + essential constraints, not HOW), with the same rule stated in discovery's STEP 2.
- **"Choices are always forms" hard rule**: `GLOBAL_CONSTRAINTS` and discovery's STEP 1/STEP 2 now state a hard rule — the instant a decision with real alternatives exists (architecture, ports, naming, defaults…), it goes to the owner as an `ask_smart_plan` form immediately, anywhere in discovery, and is NEVER enumerated as options in prose (prose is narration/findings only). STEP 2 (`plan_intent`) is now conditional: only once no decision remains open — deferring an open decision to "a form after confirmation" is explicitly forbidden, decisions that shape the objective come BEFORE the confirmation. The old "forms are encouraged"/"prose replies stay legal…" softeners that made prose-enumerated options formally legal are removed.
- **`discoveryPreIntentSteer` reordered to match**: an investigated turn with an open decision must put it to the owner as an `ask_smart_plan` form NOW, before `plan_intent` — `plan_intent` is called only once nothing is open.
- **`discoveryReadySteer` no longer claims the prior answer was "discarded and regenerated"**: the steer is delivered as a `followUp`, so the flagged turn's own answer stays in the transcript untouched; reworded to "the previous close violated the closing contract — continue by calling plan_advance now…".
- **The objective proposal is now a transcript card**: `plan_intent` appends a new `INTENT_ENTRY_KEY` entry ("OBJECTIVE PROPOSAL" — goal slug + the full statement, themed like the plan panel) to the MAIN transcript before opening its form, so the statement is durably readable there instead of only in the form's few-line side panel.
- **Confirm/Keep-chatting form no longer repeats the statement**: its `detail` is dropped now that the transcript card is the single source.
- **Objective cap enforced pre-form**: a statement over `OBJECTIVE_MAX_LEN` (~400 chars, now exported from `plan-store.ts`) is refused with `isError:true` before the transcript entry is appended or the form opens — no more sitting through the whole form only to be rejected on Confirm. `confirmIntent`'s own check stays as a belt.
- **`plan_intent` gains an explicit "Keep chatting" option**: the form is now Confirm / Correct / Keep chatting. Keep chatting — and Esc, its exact equivalent — postpones explicitly (`isError:false`, nothing created, "the owner wants to keep chatting…") instead of overloading Esc alone for "not yet".
- **Mechanical floor extended to POST-intent discovery**: the anti-prose floor previously armed only pre-intent (once investigation had started); now, once the owner has confirmed the objective via `plan_intent`, a discovery turn that closes in bare prose (no `plan_advance`/`ask_smart_plan`/`plan_intent`/`plan_save`/`journal_append`) is regenerated too, closing the gap where post-confirmation grilling questions (STEP 3) could close in unpoliced prose. Armed UNCONDITIONALLY — unlike the pre-intent gap, there's no `investigationDone` precondition, since after confirmation the phase's mandate is structured by definition. New `DISCOVERY_POST_INTENT_KEY` + `discoveryPostIntentSteer` mirror the pre-intent pair; discovery's STEP 3/grilling prompt gains a matching reminder.
- **Open questions travel as structured data, never prose**: `plan_intent` gains an optional `openQuestions` array (same trimmed question shape as `ask_smart_plan`: `question`, `header?`, `options[]` as a bare string or `{label, description}`) — a pre-confirmation safety net. Declaring it opens the SAME auto-paged form `ask_smart_plan` renders, BEFORE the objective cap, the OBJECTIVE PROPOSAL card, or the Confirm/Correct/Keep-chatting form; the store stays untouched and the result tells the model to resolve the answers and call `plan_intent` again with `openQuestions` empty (or omitted) to confirm. Every existing guard (guard-on, interactive UI, slug/statement non-empty, one-goal-per-session lock, `gateFormOpen` latch) applies to both branches; the ~400-char objective cap now applies ONLY to the confirming branch. Discovery's prompt gains a new per-turn standing order — "AT EVERY TURN — OPEN QUESTIONS VECTOR" — instructing the model to emit any open question/decision as `ask_smart_plan`'s JSON `questions` vector or, at the confirmation step, as `plan_intent.openQuestions`, never in prose; `GLOBAL_CONSTRAINTS`' "CHOICES ARE ALWAYS FORMS" rule and discovery's STEP 2, plus `discoveryPreIntentSteer`/the pre-intent `nextActionHint`, now name both channels.
- **UI/UX wave — the plan panel, tool calls and the working line all talk now, instead of raw tool names and a static "Working…"**: `renderPlanPanel`/`renderIntentPanel` move to a new pure, testable `src/render.ts` and are restyled — a labeled OBJECTIVE/SCOPE/NON-GOALS/DoD block, a hand-styled HLD body, then (once tasks exist) a style-B todo-DAG: one vertical trunk linking `● WAVE n` headers (`waits for T…–T…`) to `├─`/`└─` task branches, `☑`/`☐` done/ready/pending glyphs, the in-wave `∥` parallel marker, `← T1,T2` deps, and a `N/M done · ready now: …` footer (LIVE — re-reads the store every redraw; narrow terminals drop the `∥`/dep annotations). A leaked Italian string ("goal da definire") in the plan message renderer is fixed. Every registered tool (`plan_save`, `plan_advance`, `plan_intent`, `ask_smart_plan`, `journal_append`, `plan_recall`, `plan_next`, `plan_task_update`, `plan_verify`, `plan_exit`, `plan_complete`) now gets `renderCall`/`renderResult`: a compact `▪ <verb phrase>` call line and a themed, few-line result — colored ✓/✗ per-command rows for `plan_verify` (collapsed shows failures only; full breakdown on expand), first lines plus an expand hint for `plan_recall`; errors render in the `error` color with the failure's first line. The working-message line is now live: every phase transition sets `◈ plan · <phase> — <mission>` (the phase's own LOCAL MISSION line, read naturally), activation sets the discovery line, `execute` enriches it with `(n/m done)` progress on every `plan_task_update`, and turning the guard off or completing a goal (`plan_complete`) explicitly restores pi's default message — pi never resets it automatically between turns.
- **Gate 2 gains a "STAGED FILES PREFLIGHT" check (owner-backed defuse)**: `runReviewGate`'s `review_final` branch now computes `gitStagedFiles(ctx.cwd)` (new `plan-store.ts` helper — the same `line[0] !== " " && line[0] !== "?"` criterion pi-subagents' worker acceptance uses, which otherwise rejects EVERY write-worker while any pre-existing file is staged) before opening the Gate 2 form. When staged files are present the form's detail gains a `⚠ STAGED FILES PREFLIGHT` block listing them (partially-staged entries — both porcelain columns non-space, e.g. `MM` — flagged separately, since unstaging would drop the staged/unstaged split), and the two-option form grows a third choice: **Unstage & start implementation** (`git restore --staged -- <detected paths>` — the only git mutation this adds, scoped to exactly the detected paths — re-verified empty before proceeding; journals the unstage) alongside **Start anyway** (proceeds, journals that staged entries remain present) and **Stay in planning**. With no staged files the form stays byte-identical to before. `plan_exit`'s approved-goal kickoff message also gains a one-line staged-files warning when applicable.
- **Fixed: owns-check false-positives on parallel tasks, and its re-claim bypass**: `updateTaskStatus`'s close ("done") check used to flag a task's own dirty-file delta against ONLY that task's `owns`, so batch-claimed sibling tasks (same wave, same shared worktree) failed each other's closes on files they didn't touch — the field workaround (re-claim `in_progress` then close again) then turned out to always pass, because the dirty-file baseline lived in an in-memory `Map` that got silently overwritten on every claim, including a re-claim of an already-`in_progress` task, folding every already-dirty file into a fresh "clean" baseline. Fixed on both fronts: (1) the close check now flags a changed file only when it falls outside **every** task's declared `owns` in the plan (not just the closing task's own) — siblings' in-flight edits are tolerated, genuinely out-of-plan files still are not; the rejection message now reads "outside every task's owns". (2) The baseline is persisted per-task in `<goal>/claims.json` (same 0700 store dir as `phase.txt`/`intent.txt`), captured ONLY on a task's first claim, reused verbatim by any later re-claim (including a retry right after a rejected close), and cleared only once the task is actually closed — an abandoned goal's purge removes `claims.json` for free along with the rest of the goal dir.
- **Fixed: `ask_smart_plan`'s bare-string `options` rejected before runtime coercion could run**: the tool's TypeBox `parameters` schema required every `questions[].options` entry to be an object, so a model passing `options: ["A", "B"]` was rejected at schema validation — before `normalizeAskQuestions`'s existing bare-string-to-`{label}` coercion ever got a chance to run. `options` is now `Type.Union([Type.String(), Type.Object({label, description?, preview?})])`, matching `plan_intent.openQuestions`'s schema (already correct) and the runtime coercion that was already in place.
- **Internal simplification pass**: ~400 LOC removed across `index.ts` and `src/*.ts`, behavior-identical — includes deleting the legacy pre-0.10 phase apparatus (phase-name migration/inference) outright now that every goal directory carries a machine-owned `phase.txt`.
- **Closed the guard-off "shadow planning" gap**: `plan_save` and `plan_advance` now refuse with `plan_intent`'s own text ("plan mode is off — activate it first (shift+tab or /plan)") when the guard is off AND the target goal is still in a planning phase (`discovery`…`review_final`) — mutating the plan store or advancing the phase machine outside plan mode is exactly what the read-only guard exists to prevent. `plan_save` resolves the target goal from its params; `plan_advance` resolves it via the current pointer. Execute-phase goals are unaffected (Gate 2's guard release stays fully operational guard-off, by design). `journal_append` — append-only, never a phase/content mutation — is instead available in ANY session, guard on or off, any phase, the same as `ask_smart_plan`'s ordinary forms and `plan_recall`; its only guard is goal EXISTENCE, closing the hole where a stray call on a never-confirmed goal would otherwise silently create one via `appendJournal`'s own goal-dir bootstrap — it now refuses with `no plan named "<goal>" — goals are created by confirming an objective via plan_intent` instead.

## 0.10.0

- **Six-phase redesign, exactly two owner touchpoints**: `discovery → simplify → review_hld → decompose → review_final → execute`. Only `review_hld` (Gate 1: Approve / Reject) and `review_final` (Gate 2: Start implementation / Stay in planning) ever ask the owner anything; every other transition is the model's own job.
- **Owner-confirmed objective as a hard precondition of discovery**: new `plan_intent` tool opens a Confirm/Correct form that reformulates the owner's objective; only Confirm creates the confirmed objective (`intent.txt` — statement + timestamp) for the goal, and `plan_save` now mechanically rejects any content for the goal until `intent.txt` exists — no `plan.md` can ever exist without a confirmed objective next to it. Re-runnable in discovery to re-confirm a refined statement; locked once discovery ends and re-openable only if Gate 1 sends the plan back.
- **Four-step discovery flow**: free-form chat with proactive discovery (the model launches read-only exploration subagents on the codebase and web research on whatever the owner is discussing, in parallel with the conversation, self-assessing at the end of every turn whether the goal is identifiable) → the identified goal restated and confirmed by the owner via `plan_intent` → an optional, unbounded post-confirmation grilling round (forms and/or prose) that journals key clarifications and re-confirms via `plan_intent` if the objective materially changes → the fully refined brief presented before HLD co-design. Replaces the 0.7.0 opt-in pre-confirmation "challenge round".
- **Confirmed objective surfaced at both gates**: an `OBJECTIVE: <statement>` line now heads the live plan panel and the contract summary at both Gate 1 and Gate 2, so every approval is made against the owner's own confirmed objective.
- **New `plan_advance` tool**: formless self-advance for `discovery`/`simplify`/`decompose` — refuses (isError, phase untouched) until the current phase's deliverable is mechanically ready.
- **Harness-composed gate forms**: the two review gates' questions and option labels are generated by the extension itself, never parsed from the model's own wording — routing can't misfire regardless of the model's output language.
- **Harness-driven review gates**: calling `plan_advance` out of `simplify`/`decompose` now transitions into the review phase AND opens that phase's owner gate — panel plus form — in the very same tool call; a postponed gate re-opens via another `plan_advance` (or `ask_smart_plan` with `phaseGate: true`). This replaces the separate `plan_present` tool (now removed) and fixes a mid-run deadlock: pi applies `setActiveTools` grants only on the NEXT turn, so a model reaching `review_hld` inside one autonomous run could never see a newly-granted `plan_present` and would improvise — chat-only presentations, fabricated `journal_append` markers — instead of opening the real gate.
- **Unified tool surface across all five planning phases**: `discovery` through `review_final` now share one identical tool set, with zero mid-run tool grants or removals until `execute` starts — closing the whole class of bugs where a phase's newly-granted tool stayed invisible until the following turn.
- **Gate 1 simplified to Approve / Reject**: Reject now collects an optional owner note, journals it, and returns straight to `discovery` (previously a separate "Request changes" option handled that). Gate 2's "Start implementation" queues the authorization briefing to arrive on a FRESH turn instead of injecting it into the same tool result, so `execute` always starts with its full tool surface already active.
- **Save-time canonical-headings validation**: `plan_save` now rejects a plan immediately if it's missing `## HLD` / `## Scope` / `## Non-goals` / `## Decisions` / `## DoD` (plus `## Tasks` from `decompose` on) or has no executable DoD line, listing exactly which headings are missing and which were found — body text may still be in any language.
- **`phase.txt` authoritative over guard state**: the machine-owned phase file wins even with the guard off or re-engaged, so toggling the guard back on mid-`execute` no longer regresses the session to planning.
- **`active.txt` is the only resume path**, replacing the mtime-newest-goal heuristic outright — no fallback (0.10.0 was never published, so there are no pre-pointer stores to support); a purged or orphaned goal directory is inert.
- **Abandon-on-exit with a 10-second grace**: toggling plan mode off (`shift+tab`, `/plan-guard off`, `plan_exit`) while the active goal is still mid-planning (anywhere before `execute`) tombstones it — `active.txt` is renamed to `abandoned.txt`, so the pointer stops resolving immediately — arms a 10s in-process purge timer, and warns in chat that the plan will be discarded. Re-enabling plan mode within the window cancels the purge, restores the pointer and notifies you the plan was kept. `execute` is exempt: Gate 2's release transitions the phase before the guard drops, and re-engaging the guard mid-`execute` for supervision never tombstones anything.
- **Fresh plan-mode activations always start clean**: any tombstone left behind by a killed process is purged unconditionally on activation, and a stale `active.txt` pointer still stuck mid-planning with no tombstone at all (process killed before a grace was ever armed) is swept too. Durable approved copies (`smart-plan/approved/…`) are never touched by any of this.
- Known gap (accepted): two concurrent pi sessions planning in the same repo share this store — the fresh-activation sweep in one session can discard the other session's still-in-planning goal.
- **Reads made pure**: legacy-phase migration now happens only on write paths (first touch after upgrade) — `currentPhase`/`goalSummaries`/etc. never write to disk.
- **`plan_approve` removed** — its guard-release responsibility is folded into the `review_final` Gate 2 answer.
- **`ask_smart_plan` accepts bare-string options** (e.g. `"Yes"` alongside `{label: "Yes"}`) — no more silent schema rejection when the model passes plain strings.
- **Finalize switched to the typed `turn_end` event**, replacing the hand-rolled `agent_end` message scan; the associated type casts are removed.
- **Widget shows live context-usage percentage**; **`session_compact` posts a one-shot goal/phase/next-action recap** after compaction.

**Behavior changes:**
- Goals already mid-execution under ≤0.9 stores re-run both owner gates once on upgrade (fast — their deliverables are already complete) before the session lets them continue executing.
- Legacy phase names in existing `phase.txt` files (`hld` → `discovery`, `ablate` → `simplify`, `present` → `review_hld`) are mapped on read without rewriting the file.

## 0.9.0

- **Subagents available in plan mode, exploration-only**: `subagent` and `subagent_wait` are now in the plan-mode allowlist, so the root can delegate reconnaissance while planning.
- **Guard inheritance via env**: while the guard is active the extension exports `PI_SMART_PLAN=1`; pi-subagents children inherit the parent env, and the extension (loaded in the child) self-activates the same default-deny guard at `session_start` when `PI_SUBAGENT_DEPTH >= 1`. Nested delegation stays guarded automatically.
- **Children are exploration-only**: a dedicated child allowlist (read/bash/grep/find/ls + web research + subagent tools) excludes every plan-store tool (`plan_*`, `journal_append`, `ask_smart_plan`, `plan_exit`), so a child can never mutate the parent's shared plan store; children receive a compact read-only subagent contract instead of the phase state machine.
- **Guard-circumvention rule in global constraints**; execute phase no longer re-asks for confirmation after approval; `approvedGoals` store helper.
- Known gap (accepted): a child agent profile that declares explicit `extensions:` makes pi pass `--no-extensions`, dropping the guard from that child.

## 0.8.0

Distinct visual style (colored rule) so the final plan stands out from the rest of the chat.
- **present phase reordered**: ablation recap → human abstraction in chat → structured panel → approval form.

## 0.7.1

- **"None of the above" on every form, always last, with OPTIONAL note**: submitting an empty note now accepts the answer as-is (previously it bounced back to the list); a typed note is appended to the answer. In multi-select it is EXCLUSIVE — selecting it clears the other picks, and picking a real option clears it.
- The model is told every form automatically ends with this built-in option and must never add its own equivalent.

## 0.7.0

- **Two-level missions**: global — in plan mode the only deliverable is THE PLAN, never code/setup/implementation promises; local — every phase declares the deliverables the NEXT phase consumes, which is also its exit criterion.
- **Every question is a form**: any question to the owner — decisions, clarifications, even the opening "what do we design?" — arrives as an ask_smart_plan form with candidate directions and a custom-note escape. No prose questions, ever.
- **Challenge deep-dive inside discovery (opt-in)**: when scope + DoD are settled, a form asks whether the owner wants to CHALLENGE their implementation ideas before the HLD. If accepted: ONE challenge per turn, ALWAYS delivered as an ask_smart_plan form (never open prose) — rotating across assumptions, alternatives, failure modes, contrarian positions; check-ins every ~5 challenges, stop always available, insights journaled; the wrap-up synthesis feeds the HLD. The word "grill" is banned from all output (rounds are labeled "Challenge #N").
- **Charter fence in discovery**: proposals/charters pasted from other contexts are input to understand and challenge — never a work order; the agent builds documents, not software.

## 0.6.1

- **Lifecycle documentation**: the README now documents all six phases in detail — entry conditions, what happens, what you'll see, what's expected from you, exit conditions and written artifacts — plus the always-true rules (default-deny tools, owner-only activation, approval-gated guard release).
- **Product questions arrive as forms**: during discovery, product/decision questions go through ask_smart_plan forms (briefing + options + consequences) instead of prose; plain text is reserved for simple factual clarifications.

## 0.6.0

- **Plan mode is default-deny**: only reading (read/grep/find/ls), bash restricted to read-only commands, web research and the planning-store tools run while planning — everything else, including unknown or future third-party tools, is blocked. Writes happen exclusively in the plans folder via the store tools.
- **Human-readable plan presentations**: every presentation opens with the human abstraction (what changes, why, priorities) before the full technical DAG; the complete task list with dependencies is always posted in chat BEFORE any approval form — including fused small-goal gates.
- **Ablation recap at presentation**: the silent review phase reports one line of what it cut when the plan is shown.
- **HLD revisions journaled**: re-confirming a revised HLD records what changed versus the previous version.
- `/plan-status` zero-token state dump; `currentPhase` picks the most recently modified goal.

## 0.5.0

- **Mechanical owns verification**: claiming a task (`plan_task_update` → `in_progress`) snapshots dirty files; closing it (`done`) verifies the delta stayed inside the task's `owns` (git-backed) and that all dependencies are done. Violations reject the close and are journaled.
- **`plan_task_update` tool**: server-side checkbox flip — no more full-plan rewrites to tick a box. Dependency discipline enforced at the tool level.
- **`plan_verify` tool**: runs every DoD command of a goal's plan and reports pass/fail — mechanical delivery gate.
- **Phase-transition validation**: an explicit `phase:` line must be backed by structure (`decompose` requires HLD, `ablate`/`present` require HLD + tasks); illegal jumps reject the save.
- **Silent ablate**: the internal simplification review no longer narrates in chat; work happens through store I/O only.
- **Mandatory plan presentation**: `present` posts the complete plan in chat BEFORE any approval UI — assemble → validate → show → approve.
- **`/plan-status` command**: zero-token dump of goals, phases and frontier.
- **Smoke test ships with the repo** (`npm test`).
- `currentPhase` picks the most recently modified goal.
